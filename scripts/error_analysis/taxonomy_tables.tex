\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Consistency-Check Verdict Categories}
\label{tab:verdicts}
\small
\begin{tabular}{clp{5cm}p{4.5cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Verdict} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Example} \\
\midrule
\cmark & Consistent
  & Both documents explicitly agree on this point, or are both silent on it.
  & Paper says ``cluster at neighbourhood level''; summary says ``use neighbourhood cluster identifiers.'' \\[6pt]
\xmark & Contradicts
  & Direct conflict: document~A explicitly says X and document~B explicitly says Y, where $X \neq Y$.
  & Paper says OLS; summary says IV estimator. \\[6pt]
\omark & Omission
  & The upstream document specifies X; the downstream document is silent on X.
  & Paper states wild-cluster bootstrap; summary makes no mention of bootstrap inference. \\[6pt]
? & Unclear
  & Both documents are too vague to assess, or the relationship is genuinely ambiguous.
  & Paper uses an undefined abbreviation; summary uses a different undefined abbreviation. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\medskip
\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
\small\textit{Note:} Verdicts are assigned independently at each pipeline stage.
The upstream document is always the one earlier in the pipeline
(Stata code $\to$ paper $\to$ methodology summary $\to$ agent code).
Both \xmark\ and \omark\ trigger root-cause attribution at that stage.
\end{minipage}
\end{table}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Failure Type Taxonomy}
\label{tab:failure_types}
\begin{tabular}{llp{6cm}p{4cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Code} & \textbf{Category} & \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Example} \\
\midrule
S1 & Wrong model specification
   & Incorrect fixed effects, clustering level, or standard error type
   & Clustering on household IDs where paper specifies neighbourhood clusters \\[4pt]
S2 & Wrong estimator / inference
   & Wrong estimator (e.g.\ OLS instead of IV) or missing inference procedure
   & Wild-cluster bootstrap omitted; clustering at wrong level \\[4pt]
S3 & Data source substitution
   & Proxy or approximation used when the paper required a specific data source
   & Survey-based vote proxies instead of official machine-level electoral returns \\[4pt]
S4 & Wrong sample restriction
   & Filter missing, condition incorrect, or wrong subset of observations selected
   & Missing restriction to municipalities where incumbent ran for re-election \\[4pt]
S5 & Wrong variable construction
   & Outcome or predictor coded differently than the paper specifies
   & Binary recoding applied with incorrect thresholds or reference category \\[4pt]
S6 & Missing analysis component
   & A required step entirely omitted
   & No code implementing Table~4 electoral-outcomes analysis \\[4pt]
S8 & Wrong data transformation / merge logic
   & Incorrect join type, wrong merge key, mishandled duplicates, or flawed reshape
   & Left join used instead of inner join, retaining unmatched observations \\[4pt]
S9 & Wrong sequencing / order of operations
   & Steps performed in incorrect order, changing results even if each step is individually correct
   & Winsorizing applied before merge instead of after \\[4pt]
S0 & Other
   & A substantive difference that does not fit any category above
   & (described in notes) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
